# Howto

Sign in to Northern first (`/fs/get/reg/Reg.html`); a visitor who is not signed in is sent there and back.

Test in local:
```
https://localhost:9443/fs/get/pwa/zine/index.html
https://localhost:9443/fs/get/pwa/zine/index.html?issue=current
```

A new issue `<name>` is two files: `maps/<name>.png` (the floor) and `objects/<name>.json` (what stands on it - see `objects/current.json`).
